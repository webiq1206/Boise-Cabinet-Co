# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Public catalog and product discovery pages
- Public guides, blog, testimonials, resources, contact, about, warranty pages
- Crawlability assets such as `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, favicon, and manifest

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard and portal areas (`/admin/**`, `/portal/**`, `/subcontractor/**`)
- Login and internal utility flows not intended to rank (`/login`, `/search`, `/design-studio`, API routes)

## Target audience
- Treasure Valley homeowners researching custom cabinets, remodeling, and built-in storage
- Potential dealer, installer, and partner prospects are secondary and mostly non-index targets

## Primary keywords
- Boise custom cabinets
- Kitchen cabinets Boise
- Bathroom cabinets Boise
- Cabinet company Boise
- Treasure Valley custom cabinets
