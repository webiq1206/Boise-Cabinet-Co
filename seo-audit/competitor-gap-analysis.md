# Competitor Gap Analysis - Boise Cabinet Co

Strongest organic competitors for "custom cabinets Boise / Treasure Valley":

| Competitor | Their edge | vs BCC |
|---|---|---|
| **Sweetwood Custom Cabinets** | 45 yrs (est. 1979), named owners (Brian & Parker Sweet), `/our-team/`, 5.0 Houzz / 5.0 Angi / 4.7 (46) - the E-E-A-T benchmark | Crushes BCC on trust fundamentals; BCC has none live |
| **Western Idaho Cabinets** | Physical Boise showroom, "largest cabinet shop" claim, category galleries | Beats BCC on physical presence/NAP; BCC wins on process + estimator + content. Winnable |
| **Treasure Valley Custom Cabinets** | Exact-match domain, veteran/family positioning | Owns the literal keyword via domain; BCC must win on depth + reviews |
| **Weckwerth Cabinets** | Since 2001, luxury positioning, Houzz + Angi | Longer tenure; BCC counters with Design Studio/portal/estimator |

**Picture:** BCC is **ahead on digital sophistication** (token-driven site, Design Studio, client portal, estimator, deep guides/blog, structured data, no-pressure messaging) but **behind on the fundamentals ranking rewards** - real reviews, named humans, provable tenure, verified GBP, authentic project photography.

## Prioritized gaps to close
**P0 (before promotion):** verify + link GBP and collect 25+ reviews; name the team (photos + credentials + Person schema); replace stock imagery with real project photos; publish license/bond/insurance.
**P1 (parity):** above-the-fold trust bar; named author on all content; fix testimonials (name + city + date + source); claim Houzz/Yelp/BBB.
**P2 (pull ahead):** transparent cost pages (competitors hide pricing - BCC's estimator is a real differentiator); years-in-business + project-count stats; NKBA/mfr certifications; per-service + city trust modules.

**Bottom line:** BCC's opportunity is that its top competitors have strong trust but weak digital experience and thin content. Populate the trust data it already has slots for, and it pairs incumbent-level trust with a best-in-class site.
