# Doorway Page Analysis - Boise Cabinet Co

Evaluated against Google's doorway-page guidance (multiple similar pages targeting the same intent, funneling to one destination, low standalone value).

- **382 product SKU pages:** textbook near-duplicate within category (>=95% similar), BUT `noindex,follow` + excluded from the sitemap + self-canonical. **Not a doorway problem in practice** - correctly de-indexed while kept as internal configurator utilities.
- **106 long-tail/duplicate finishes:** `noindex,follow`, canonical to the category. Handled.
- **90 indexable woodgrain finishes:** share 100% identical prose; only name + hex + swatch differ. **This is the one indexable set that still reads doorway-shaped.** Action: consolidate to a gallery, index ~15 with unique copy.
- **8 city guides:** genuinely differentiated (named subdivisions, county permit sections, unique FAQs). **Explicitly NOT doorways** - the model for how to do local programmatic pages.
- **Blog default-fallback posts:** thin and >=70% similar to each other where they fall back to generic sections - a thin-content/Helpful-Content risk more than a classic doorway.

**Net:** doorway exposure is limited to the 90 woodgrain finishes (merge) and the thin blog tail (expand). Everything else is either correctly de-indexed or genuinely unique.
