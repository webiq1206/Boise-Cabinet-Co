# Content Gap Analysis - Boise Cabinet Co

Prioritized by impact. BCC's content **breadth** is strong (8 hubs, 56 posts, 17 guides, 8 city guides, cost clusters); the gaps are **depth, proof, and a few high-intent page types.**

1. **[HIGH] Indexable project/case-study pages (`/projects/[slug]`).** The 3 detailed case studies (real budgets, timelines, neighborhoods) render **only on the homepage** - no route. This is the site's best first-hand E-E-A-T content, undiscoverable. Build project pages linking project -> room -> city -> finish, with real before/after photos.
2. **[HIGH] Comparison / decision pages.** Missing: custom vs stock/big-box (cost + quality), frameless vs framed, refacing vs replacement, a door-style comparison table. High commercial intent, convert well, and feed AI decision queries.
3. **[HIGH] Blog depth.** 56 posts at median 217 words -> expand to 700-1,200 with first-hand specifics; merge the 3 overlapping outdoor posts.
4. **[MEDIUM] Process / expectation pages.** Standalone "how we measure", "installation-day walkthrough", "lead time / what to expect" with photos (the process guide exists but is thin).
5. **[MEDIUM] Finish pairing lookbooks.** Replace the 90 thin woodgrain pages with a handful of "finish + countertop + hardware" lookbooks with real photography - absorbs the long-tail without doorway risk.
6. **[MEDIUM] City project galleries.** Extend the (excellent) city guides with real local before/after photos + per-city reviews rather than adding new city permutations.
7. **[MEDIUM] Bathroom-vanities cluster** - under-built for a core service (4 clusters, 1 room); target ~8.
8. **[LOW] Cost depth** - largely covered by the WAVE1 cost clusters; just expand the thin ones.

**Quotable-fact gaps (feeds GEO/AEO):** finish/room/door detail pages carry zero numbers - inject typical lead time, warranty term, and a starting price band as self-contained sentences.
