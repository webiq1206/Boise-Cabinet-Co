I want to design a back-end system for a lead distribution platform that allows me (the admin) to manage incoming leads and distribute them to subcontractors. This system must also integrate with QuoteWizard, so that any leads submitted through QuoteWizard automatically populate the internal lead dashboard.

1. Lead Intake & Admin Flow:
	•	When a new lead is submitted—either through the website or from QuoteWizard—I (the admin) receive an immediate notification.
	•	I have first rights of refusal on every lead:
	•	If I choose to accept the lead, it is marked as claimed by me and not shared further.
	•	If I decline it, the lead is added to a dashboard of available leads for subcontractors.

2. Subcontractor Notifications & Lead Access:
	•	All registered subcontractors are notified by email when a new lead becomes available.
	•	Each lead has an associated purchase cost, which varies depending on project type:
	•	For one-time jobs: 10% of the total quoted amount.
	•	For recurring jobs (e.g., lawn maintenance): the lead price equals the cost of one standard service visit.
	•	When a subcontractor purchases a lead, they immediately gain access to full contact details and the property address.
	•	Leads not purchased will reduce in price automatically by 1–2% each day until purchased.
	•	Subcontractors should also have the option to purchase leads in bulk.

3. Lead Browsing & Filtering:
	•	Subcontractors must be able to browse and filter available leads by:
	•	Project type
	•	Lead cost
	•	Expected revenue
	•	Other relevant filters

4. Legal Agreement Before Access:
	•	Before any subcontractor can access full lead information, they must electronically sign an agreement.
	•	The agreement should:
	•	Confirm the lead is not a guaranteed job.
	•	Release Lawn Care Kuna of all liability.
	•	Include a clear no-refund policy for lead purchases.

5. QuoteWizard Integration:
	•	The back-end system must connect to the QuoteWizard API or data feed.
	•	Any leads submitted on QuoteWizard should automatically be imported into the internal system and follow the same flow described above (admin first, then subcontractors if declined).
